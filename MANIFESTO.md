# TM-0 Manifesto

TM-0 is the smallest executable hypothesis of Thalean Mechanics.

It is an experiment.

Nothing in TM-0 is assumed because it is beautiful.

Nothing is retained because it is familiar.

Every concept must earn its existence through necessity.

TM-0 does not begin with:

- space
- time
- matter
- energy
- information
- observers
- graphs
- geometry
- probability
- agency

TM-0 begins with possibility.

Possibility is Null with character.

Null is uncharacterized possibility.

Character is accumulated registration across completed lawful cycles.

The primitive cycle is

    Possibility
        ->
    Relation
        ->
    Closure
        ->
    Return
        ->
    Registration
        ->
    Character
        ->
    Possibility

Action is not primitive.

Action is the realization of one possibility.

Identity is not primitive.

Identity must emerge.

Motion is not primitive.

Motion must emerge.

Geometry is not primitive.

Geometry must emerge.

Graphs are not primitive.

Graphs must emerge.

The purpose of TM-0 is to discover the smallest mechanics capable of producing stable relational character.

If larger structures eventually emerge naturally, they are witnesses.

They are not assumptions.

TM-0 protects no conclusions.

TM-0 protects only the process by which conclusions earn the right to exist.
