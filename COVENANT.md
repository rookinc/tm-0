# TM-0 Covenant

TM-0 exists to discover.

TM-0 does not exist to confirm.

The following rules govern every experiment.

---

Rule 1

No primitive survives without necessity.

---

Rule 2

No new noun enters the ontology until an experiment requires it.

---

Rule 3

No theorem is protected.

Every theorem must survive repeated attempts to eliminate it.

---

Rule 4

Every implementation must correspond to an ontological commitment.

No hidden mechanics.

---

Rule 5

Every experiment produces a receipt.

Negative results are first-class research.

---

Rule 6

Every successful experiment should simplify the ontology.

Complexity is a cost.

---

Rule 7

If two explanations exist, prefer the one requiring fewer primitive assumptions.

---

Rule 8

Graphs, geometry, probability, identity, time, and agency are not primitive.

They must earn their existence.

---

Rule 9

TM-0 is not a simulation.

TM-0 is an executable ontology.

---

Rule 10

Do not protect the idea.

Make the idea protect itself.
