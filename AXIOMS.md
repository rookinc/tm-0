# TM-0 Axioms

The following axioms define the smallest known mechanics under investigation.

Nothing below is considered proven.

Each axiom exists only until it can be derived from a more primitive statement.

---

Axiom 0

Possibility exists.

TM-0 begins with possibility.

Null is possibility without accumulated character.

---

Axiom 1

Possibility admits relation.

No relation may exist independently of possibility.

---

Axiom 2

Relations may achieve closure.

Closure creates a body capable of lawful return.

---

Axiom 3

Closure admits return.

Return is impossible without closure.

---

Axiom 4

Return admits registration.

Completed lawful return leaves residue.

---

Axiom 5

Registration accumulates as character.

Character is accumulated registration across completed lawful cycles.

---

Axiom 6

Character shapes possibility.

Character does not determine possibility.

Character gives possibility structure.

---

Axiom 7

Action realizes possibility.

Action is the realization of one structured possibility.

Completed action contributes to future character.

---

Research Rule

Every future theorem should eliminate or derive one axiom.

The objective of TM-0 is to reduce the number of primitive assumptions.
