# Receipt 0034

## Title

Reliability Is Earned by Later Closure

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Witness reliability can accumulate through repeated comparison between:

    earlier witness report

and:

    later closure receipt

Agreement adds confirmation.

Disagreement adds contradiction.

A witness with no evaluated receipts has no earned reliability.

## Interpretation

Reliability is not granted when a witness speaks.

Reliability emerges from the history of whether later closure confirms
or contradicts prior reports.

## Accumulated State

The tested witness history records:

    confirmed reports

    contradicted reports

The current toy reliability value is:

    confirmed - contradicted

## Consequence

Witness arbitration may eventually use:

    independent provenance

    accumulated reliability

rather than:

    report count alone

This moves witness weight from assertion toward accountable history.

## Limitation

The reliability score is a scaffold.

The experiment assumes that the later closure receipt is the correct
reference.

It does not yet model:

    uncertain closure

    partial agreement

    shared error

    forged receipts

    reliability decay

    context-specific reliability

## Keeper

A witness does not begin trusted.

Later closure gives the report a history.
