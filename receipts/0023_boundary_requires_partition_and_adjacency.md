# Receipt 0023

## Title

Boundary Requires Partition and Adjacency

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Character can partition the possibility address space into:

    ADMISSIBLE

    STRUCTURED_ABSENCE

That partition creates distinction.

It does not by itself create a boundary set.

A boundary appears only when addresses from opposite classes are
adjacent.

## Interpretation

Structured absence is not the boundary.

Structured absence is one side of the distinction.

Boundary is the meeting relation between:

    admissible possibility

and:

    structured absence

## Necessary Ingredients

The tested model requires:

    character-induced classification

    address adjacency

    cross-class contact

Without adjacency, the partition remains real but no boundary address
is visible.

## Consequence

The current ladder is:

    character
        -> classification

    classification
        -> admissible and structured absence

    adjacency
        -> possible contact

    cross-class adjacency
        -> boundary

## Limitation

Adjacency remains assumed.

The experiment does not yet derive why possibility addresses should be
adjacent.

It does not derive boundary persistence, body, resolution, selection,
time, geometry, or physical motion.

## Keeper

Distinction gives the sides.

Adjacency gives the boundary.
