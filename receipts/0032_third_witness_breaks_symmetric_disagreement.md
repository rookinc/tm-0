# Receipt 0032

## Title

Third Witness Breaks Symmetric Disagreement

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Two equal witness reports reach consensus.

Two conflicting witness reports remain unresolved when neither has
earned priority.

A third report can resolve the conflict by strict majority.

## Interpretation

The triad is not required for return.

It first becomes necessary in the tested model at the arbitration
layer.

The three roles are:

    report

    counter-report

    deciding witness

## Consequence

The current minimums separate cleanly:

    two states
        enough for nontrivial return

    three witness reports
        enough to resolve one binary disagreement

This supports the idea that triadic structure emerges from witness
stability rather than from closure itself.

## Limitation

The strict-majority law remains supplied.

A majority is not automatically true.

The experiment does not establish:

    witness independence

    reliability

    provenance

    truth

    immunity to coordinated error

## Keeper

Return needs two states.

Symmetric disagreement needs a third witness.
