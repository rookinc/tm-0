# Receipt 0016

## Title

Cycle Sign Product Is the Return Residue

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

A closed relation cycle may carry one local orientation sign per
relation:

    PRESERVE = +1

    INVERT = -1

The returned registration is determined by the product of those signs.

    product = +1 -> SAME

    product = -1 -> POLAR

## Interpretation

Cycle length alone does not determine returned polarity.

Odd length produces polarity only when the local sign assignment has
negative product.

The general closure residue is:

    Sigma(C) = product of local signs on C

## Consequence

The half-flip is no longer modeled as a special operation applied at
closure.

It may appear as the global receipt of local orientation character
composed around a closed path.

## Limitation

The local sign assignment remains unexplained.

The experiment does not yet derive:

    why a relation preserves or inverts

    whether signs obey a cocycle law

    whether sign assignments are gauge-dependent

    whether the cycle product is switching-invariant

## Keeper

Local character composes.

Closure makes its product readable.
