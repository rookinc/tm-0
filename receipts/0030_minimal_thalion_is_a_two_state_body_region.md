# Receipt 0030

## Title

Minimal Thalion Is a Two-State Body Region

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

The smallest tested body region that supports nontrivial character
change while preserving one boundary contains:

    two character states

    one persistent boundary

    one boundary-preserving transition

A one-state region does not qualify.

Two connected states with identical character do not qualify.

A boundary-changing transition does not enlarge the candidate.

## Candidate Definition

    thalion
        =
    minimal nontrivial boundary-preserving character region

In the tested finite system, minimal means two states.

## Interpretation

A thalion is not one static state.

It is the smallest body capable of carrying character through an
internal realization without losing its boundary.

## Limitation

The criteria remain candidate criteria.

The experiment does not yet derive:

    closure inside the thalion

    return inside the thalion

    A/B registration

    witness

    triadic stabilization

It does not prove that every lawful TM system has the same minimum.

## Consequence

The thalion has entered TM-0 as an executable candidate rather than an
assumed object.

The next squeeze is whether a two-state body region can internally
support closure and return, or whether more structure is required.

## Keeper

A thalion begins where one boundary can survive a real change of
character.
