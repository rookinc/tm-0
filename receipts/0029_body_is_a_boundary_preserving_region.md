# Receipt 0029

## Title

Body Is a Boundary-Preserving Region

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

A finite family of character states can form one body region when the
states are connected through boundary-preserving realizations.

A state reached only through boundary change lies outside that body
region.

## Interpretation

A body is not one frozen character state.

A body is the connected region of character variation that preserves
one boundary signature.

This separates:

    internal change

from:

    body change

## Candidate Definition

    body
        =
    connected boundary-preserving character region

Within that region, return character may change while the inside and
outside distinction persists.

## Consequence

Body continuity no longer requires static character.

It requires a connected path of admissible internal realizations that
preserve boundary.

Boundary change leaves the current body region and requires a new
classification.

## Limitation

The tested region is finite and supplied by the apparatus.

The experiment does not yet derive the full transition system from the
possibility frontier.

It does not classify boundary change as growth, division, merger,
repair, decay, or failure.

It does not yet derive the thalion.

## Keeper

The body persists where character can change without losing the
boundary.
