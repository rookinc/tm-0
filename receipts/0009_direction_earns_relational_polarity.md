# Receipt 0009

## Title

Direction Earns Relational Polarity

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Two carriers with identical internal histories and identical
undirected neighborhoods can occupy distinct directed roles:

    source

    target

The distinction disappears when direction is forgotten.

## Interpretation

Polarity has re-entered the model for a reason.

It is not yet:

    intrinsic A/B character

It is:

    outgoing/incoming relational role

## Preserved Symmetry

Reordering neighbors does not create a distinction.

Perfect duplicate directed profiles still collapse.

## Consequence

Directed relation supplies a minimal asymmetry without assigning
names, IDs, coordinates, or intrinsic polarity.

A/B remains unearned as an internal register.

Source/target role is now an earned relational distinction.

## Keeper

Polarity first appears in relation, not inside the isolated carrier.
