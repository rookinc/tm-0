# Receipt 0008

## Title

Relation Can Distinguish Identical Histories

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Two carriers with identical continuity traces can be distinguished
when they participate in different relational neighborhoods.

The distinction does not require:

    a name

    an identifier

    a coordinate

    a timestamp

    intrinsic carrier identity

## Preserved Symmetry

Neighbor ordering does not create a distinction.

Only relational content and multiplicity matter in the tested model.

## Limitation

Perfect duplicate carriers with perfect duplicate relational profiles
still collapse.

Relation therefore distinguishes relationally different instances,
but does not produce absolute individuation.

## Consequence

Identity may begin as stable relational position rather than as an
intrinsic token.

This earns relation as a candidate mechanism for distinction, not yet
as a primitive substance.

## Keeper

A carrier may be anonymous in itself and distinct in relation.
