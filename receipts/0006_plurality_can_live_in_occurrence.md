# Receipt 0006

## Title

Plurality Can Live in Occurrence

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Two lawful null-carrier instantiations can be counted as two
occurrences even when their resulting carrier states remain
extensionally identical.

The ledger preserves:

    occurrence count = 2

The carrier catalogue preserves:

    distinct carrier description count = 1

## Interpretation

Plurality need not initially belong to carrier identity.

It may first appear as multiplicity in realization history.

## Limitation

Occurrence multiplicity does not yet provide enduring individuation.

After the ledger is discarded, the two resulting null-carrier states
remain indistinguishable under the current lawful description.

## Consequence

Occurrence is a candidate bridge between:

    one anonymous carrier form

and:

    repeated realizations of that form

It has not yet earned the stronger claim:

    two persistent thalions

## Keeper

Plurality may occur before identity persists.
