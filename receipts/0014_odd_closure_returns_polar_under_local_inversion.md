# Receipt 0014

## Title

Odd Closure Returns Polar Under Local Inversion

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

One uniform local inversion at every traversed relation produces:

    even closed chain -> SAME

    odd closed chain  -> POLAR

The tested five-relation and fifteen-relation closures both return
with opposite orientation.

## Interpretation

Global polarity can emerge from composition.

It need not be assigned by a special global flip at closure.

Under the tested local law:

    returned orientation = departure orientation times (-1)^n

where n is the number of local inversions.

## Limitation

The local inversion law remains assumed.

The experiment has not shown why every traversed relation should
invert orientation.

## Consequence

The original step-five intuition now has a conditional executable
form:

    closure admits return

    local inversions compose during return

    odd composition produces polarity

The half-flip may therefore be a global receipt of local inversion
parity rather than a special operation applied only at closure.

## Keeper

An odd return does not need a special final flip.

Its polarity can be the receipt of every local flip along the way.
