# Receipt 0036

## Title

Reliability Belongs to Context

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

One witness can accumulate different reliability histories in
different contexts.

A confirmation in one context does not raise reliability in another.

A contradiction in one context does not lower reliability in another.

An unseen context begins with no earned reliability.

## Interpretation

Witness reliability is not necessarily one global scalar.

It may be a structured field indexed by the relational conditions in
which reports were made and later evaluated.

## Consequence

Reliability-weighted arbitration should eventually depend on:

    witness

    current context

    context-specific closure history

A witness may carry:

    strong reliability here

    weak reliability elsewhere

    no earned reliability in a new context

## Limitation

Context labels remain supplied by the experiment.

The model does not yet derive context from:

    cycle-space character

    boundary signature

    observation path

    provenance structure

It does not model similarity between contexts, reliability transfer,
uncertainty, decay, or forged receipts.

## Keeper

Reliability is earned somewhere.

It does not automatically follow the witness everywhere.
