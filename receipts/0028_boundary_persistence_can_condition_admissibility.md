# Receipt 0028

## Title

Boundary Persistence Can Condition Admissibility

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

Candidate realizations can be partitioned by whether they preserve the
current boundary set.

A candidate that preserves the boundary is admitted.

A candidate that changes the boundary is placed in structured absence.

## Interpretation

Admissibility can be conditioned by structural persistence rather than
by an arbitrary scalar score.

The tested rule asks whether the current distinction between:

    admissible

and:

    structured absence

survives the candidate realization.

## Consequence

Character may change while boundary remains stable.

This separates:

    internal character update

from:

    boundary change

The first may be compatible with persistence.

The second may require a different interpretation.

## Important Limitation

Boundary change is not necessarily unlawful.

It may later represent:

    growth

    repair

    division

    merger

    decay

    failure

Experiment 028 tests only one persistence law.

It does not establish that stable boundary is the universal condition
for admissibility.
