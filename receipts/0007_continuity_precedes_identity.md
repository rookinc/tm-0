# Receipt 0007

## Title

Continuity Precedes Identity

## Status

Research receipt.

## Result

An anonymous carrier history can remain referable across lawful
realizations when each later trace contains the earlier trace as a
prefix.

Example:

    NULL
        ->
    NULL, CHARACTERIZED
        ->
    NULL, CHARACTERIZED, NULL

The later trace preserves the earlier lineage without an assigned ID.

## Limitation

Two independently produced traces with identical state histories
remain extensionally equal.

Continuity therefore preserves:

    lineage through change

but does not yet preserve:

    plurality among identical lineages

## Consequence

Identity need not be primitive for one continuing history.

Additional structure is still required to distinguish two carriers
whose complete histories are identical.

## Keeper

Continuity can preserve a carrier through change.

Continuity alone cannot separate perfect duplicates.
