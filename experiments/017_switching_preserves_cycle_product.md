# Experiment 017

## Title

Switching Preserves the Cycle Product

## Status

Executable candidate.

## Question

Are local relation signs absolute, or can they change under local
re-reading while preserving the closed-cycle return residue?

## Starting Result

Experiment 016 established:

    cycle sign product = +1 -> SAME

    cycle sign product = -1 -> POLAR

The local sign assignment itself remained unexplained.

## Local Switching Rule

Each junction carries one local switch:

    +1 = preserve local reading

    -1 = reverse local reading

For a relation from junction i to junction i+1:

    sign_prime(i)
        =
    switch(i) * sign(i) * switch(i+1)

## Result

A local switch may change the signs of adjacent relations.

The product of all signs around the closed cycle remains unchanged.

A positive cycle remains positive.

A negative cycle remains negative.

A global switch at every junction changes no relation sign.

## Interpretation

Individual local signs are not absolute under the tested switching
rule.

The closed-cycle sign product is invariant.

This makes the return residue more fundamental than any one local sign
description.

## Consequence

Local signs may be gauge-like readings.

The closed-cycle product is the invariant receipt visible after return.

## Boundary

This experiment does not derive the switching rule.

It does not prove that all equivalent sign assignments are physically
or mechanically identical.

It does not yet derive a cocycle, cohomology class, character update,
witness, or thalion.

## Keeper

Local signs may change with the reading.

The return product does not.
