# Experiment 008

## Title

Relational Position Without Intrinsic Identity

## Status

Executable candidate.

## Question

Can two carriers with extensionally identical continuity traces become
distinguishable through relational participation alone?

## Starting Result

Experiment 007 showed that continuity preserves lineage through change
but cannot distinguish perfect duplicate histories.

## Construction

A relational profile contains:

    center continuity trace

    multiset of neighboring continuity traces

No names, identifiers, coordinates, timestamps, or intrinsic identity
tokens are admitted.

## Tests

The experiment compares identical center histories under:

    different neighbor counts

    equal neighbor counts with different neighbor histories

    reordered but otherwise identical neighborhoods

    perfectly duplicate relational profiles

## Expected Result

Identical carrier histories should become distinguishable when their
relational neighborhoods differ.

Changing only neighbor order should not create a distinction.

Perfect duplicate histories with perfect duplicate neighborhoods
should still collapse.

## Interpretation

Relational position can distinguish otherwise identical carriers
without assigning intrinsic identity.

The distinction belongs to relational structure rather than to the
carrier considered in isolation.

## Boundary

This experiment does not establish enduring identity under changes to
the neighborhood.

It does not derive directed relation, polarity, closure, return,
witness, character accumulation, geometry, or graph structure.

It tests only immediate anonymous relational profile.
