# Experiment 009

## Title

Directed Relational Role

## Status

Executable candidate.

## Question

Can directed relation distinguish otherwise identical carriers when
their undirected relational profiles are identical?

## Starting Result

Experiment 008 showed that anonymous relational neighborhoods can
distinguish identical continuity traces when their neighborhoods
differ.

Perfect duplicate undirected profiles still collapse.

## Construction

A directed relational profile contains:

    center continuity trace

    incoming neighbor traces

    outgoing neighbor traces

No names, identifiers, coordinates, timestamps, intrinsic A/B labels,
or implementation identity are admitted.

## Core Comparison

The experiment compares:

    center -> neighbor

with:

    neighbor -> center

From the center's perspective these become:

    outgoing role

and:

    incoming role

Their undirected shadows are identical.

Their directed profiles are not.

## Expected Result

Direction should distinguish source and target roles even when:

    center histories are identical

    neighbor histories are identical

    undirected neighborhoods are identical

Perfect duplicate directed profiles should still collapse.

## Interpretation

Directed relation may earn the first relational polarity:

    source

    target

This polarity belongs to participation in a relation.

It is not yet an intrinsic A/B state of the carrier.

## Boundary

This experiment does not derive a global orientation.

It does not establish that every relation is directed.

It does not derive return, comparison, registration character,
witness, closure, geometry, graph structure, or persistent absolute
identity.
