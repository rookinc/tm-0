# Experiment 006

## Title

Anonymous Occurrence Plurality

## Status

Executable candidate.

## Question

Can TM-0 represent two null-carrier realizations without assigning
names, persistent identities, coordinates, relations, or distinct
carrier descriptions?

## Starting Result

Experiment 005 showed that:

    Carrier(NULL)
    Carrier(NULL)

collapse to one extensional carrier description.

Register value alone does not preserve plurality.

## Construction

Experiment 006 records instantiation occurrences:

    occurrence -> Carrier(NULL)

Each occurrence has the same resulting carrier description.

The occurrence ledger preserves multiplicity:

    [occurrence, occurrence]

while the resulting carrier catalogue collapses to:

    [Carrier(NULL)]

## Result Sought

The model should simultaneously show:

    occurrence count = 2

    distinct carrier description count = 1

This would place plurality in realization history rather than in
carrier state.

## Interpretation

Occurrence is weaker than identity.

It records that realization happened more than once.

It does not establish that the resulting carriers remain distinct,
individuated, addressable, or related.

## Boundary

This experiment does not derive persistent identity.

It does not prove that two enduring thalions exist.

It does not introduce names, timestamps, coordinates, provenance,
relations, boundaries, character, return, or witness.

It tests only whether anonymous history can preserve multiplicity.
