# TM-0 Vocabulary

This document defines the primitive terms of TM-0.

No term may depend on a later term.

Every later term must be derivable from earlier ones.

---

Null

Uncharacterized possibility.

Null is not emptiness.

Null has no accumulated character.

---

Possibility

Null endowed with character.

Possibility is the frontier of lawful continuations.

Possibility does not determine action.

---

Relation

The smallest admissible distinction.

Relations carry accumulated character.

---

Closure

The completion of a relation into a body capable of return.

Closure is the condition that permits return.

---

Return

A lawful traversal of a completed relation.

Return is not motion.

Return is the first recursive act.

---

Registration

The residue produced by comparing completed return.

Registration persists beyond the completed action.

---

Character

Accumulated registration across completed lawful cycles.

Character shapes possibility.

Character does not determine possibility.

---

Thalion

The smallest recursively stable vessel capable of carrying relational character through repeated lawful cycles.
