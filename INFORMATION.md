# Information

TM-0 Working Definition

Status

Research hypothesis.

Not theorem.

---

Traditional information theories begin with symbols, messages, or states.

TM-0 begins with possibility.

The working hypothesis of TM-0 is:

    Information is the evolution of lawful possibility under accumulated constraint.

This definition is intentionally ontological rather than communicative.

It does not begin with observers.

It does not begin with language.

It does not begin with measurement.

It begins with becoming.

---

Primitive Cycle

    Null
        ->
    Possibility
        ->
    Realization
        ->
    Constraint
        ->
    Possibility

Character is the accumulated local expression of constraint.

Constraint shapes future possibility.

Information is the evolution of that lawful possibility.

---

Interpretation

Possibility is the substrate.

Realization changes possibility.

Every realization leaves residue.

Accumulated residue becomes constraint.

Constraint reshapes future possibility.

The continual lawful evolution of possibility under accumulated constraint is information.

---

Consequences

Information is not a thing.

Information is not a message.

Information is not stored bits.

Information is a process.

Objects may carry information.

Graphs may encode information.

Language may describe information.

None of these are information itself.

---

Open Questions

Can relation be derived from realization?

Can constraint be derived from repeated realization?

Can character be derived from accumulated constraint?

Can stability emerge without assuming identity?

Can G15 emerge from TM-0?
